### **Optimization Rules:**

## **BEST PRACTICES**

### **Do's:**
✅ Reference their specific details (goal, budget, timeline)
✅ Provide value before asking for anything
✅ Make it easy to respond (yes/no questions)
✅ Be human and conversational
✅ Follow up 3-5 times before giving up

### **Don'ts:**
❌ Send generic copy-paste emails
❌ Be pushy or aggressive
❌ Send more than 2 emails per week
❌ Use salesy language or hype
❌ Forget to include an unsubscribe option
