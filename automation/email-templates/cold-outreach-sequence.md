• Capture and follow up with leads automatically
• Eliminate repetitive manual work
• Give you back 10+ hours/week

Jeffery Addae

**Why This Works:**
- Personalized to their company
- Identifies a specific pain point
- Shows clear ROI (time + money saved)
- Low-friction ask (15 minutes)
- P.S. with video increases engagement by 40%

Best,
Jeffery

**Why This Works:**
- Acknowledges their time

- Humor breaks tension
- Three clear options (easy to reply)
- Creates urgency (last email)

## **COLD EMAIL BEST PRACTICES**

### **Technical Setup:**
1. **Warm up your email domain** (14 days minimum)
2. **Send from personal email** (not info@/hello@)
3. **Max 50 emails/day** from new domain
4. **Personalize first line** (AI can help)
5. **Test subject lines** (A/B test everything)

### **Timing:**
- **Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best times:** 10am-11am or 2pm-3pm (recipient's timezone)
- **Avoid:** Mondays (busy), Fridays (checked out), weekends

### **Metrics to Track:**
- **Open rate:** 40-60% = good
- **Reply rate:** 5-15% = excellent
- **Booked calls:** 2-5% = crushing it
- **Closed deals:** 0.5-2% = profitable

### **Pro Tips:**
1. **Keep it under 150 words** (people skim)
2. **One CTA only** (don't confuse them)
3. **Write like a human** (not a robot)
4. **Use "you" 3x more than "I"**
5. **Test everything** (subject lines, CTAs, length)

## **LEGAL COMPLIANCE (CAN-SPAM Act)**

✅ **Always include:**
- Your real name and company
- Physical business address (can be PO box)
- Clear unsubscribe option ("reply STOP")
- Accurate subject lines (no deceptive claims)

✅ **Honor opt-outs within 10 days**
